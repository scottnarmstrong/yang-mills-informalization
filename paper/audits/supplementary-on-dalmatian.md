# Fresh-reader audit: Part I and Part II opening

Audit basis: rendered `paper/main.pdf`, pages 2–18, checked against source locations below. I read these pages as a mathematician who had only read the preceding pages of this paper. Findings are numbered individually; exact words are quoted.

1. Part I, The result, `paper/part1/01-result.tex:12`: “called phases” (U) names the components before the paper defines what a phase is or how a component correlation is obtained; give the idea here or introduce the components before naming them.
2. Part I, The result, `paper/part1/01-result.tex:13`: “positive energy, relativistic covariance and commutativity at spacelike separation” (J) lists three technical properties without saying in ordinary terms what each says; the link to Definition 2.16 does not orient a first-time reader.
3. 4. Part I, The result, `paper/part1/01-result.tex:15`: “finite free-field expressions specified below” (U) postpones the meaning of the comparison class; state what kind of expressions these are or give a direct forward reference to Definition 2.22.
5. Part I, The result, `paper/part1/01-result.tex:24`: “the fixed formal-source revision” (J) is unexplained project/source jargon and does not tell the reader anything about the mathematical statements; identify the source in reader-facing terms or remove this sentence.
6. Part I, Definitions introduction, `paper/part1/02-definitions.tex:4`: “decomposition into phases” (U) is promised before “phase” is defined; briefly explain that the lattice-limit correlations may be averages of component-state correlations.
7. Part I, Definitions introduction, `paper/part1/02-definitions.tex:5`: “the two short-distance tests” (U) does not identify which tests or what they test; name the product estimate and trace comparison, or explain their roles.
